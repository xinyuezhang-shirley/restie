# Art for Rusty's life: trips, souvenirs, postcards

The app already works without these. The door sign is drawn in code, and souvenirs use a generic sticker. These
images make the trips feel real. Make them in this order: the sign and souvenirs first, since they're the ones you
see most.

Style reference for every prompt: attach `art/visual-refresh/room/room-day.png` (the room) and, for anything with
Rusty in it, `art/puppet/neutral.webp` (his look). If your tool can't make a transparent background, ask for flat
pure white and I'll cut it out.

Save everything in `art/life/` with the file names below, then tell me.

---

## 1. `door-sign.png`: the sign on his door while he's out

Attach `room-day.png`.

```text
A small hand-made door hanger sign in exactly the watercolor style of the
attached room: soft paper texture, warm honey wood, thin brown outlines, muted
colors. A rectangle of cream card with slightly rounded corners, a thin wooden
frame, hanging from a loop of brown twine. Leave the card EMPTY: no words, no
letters (the app writes on it). A tiny paw print stamp in the bottom-right
corner. Seen straight on. Transparent background, no shadow, no wall, no door.
Card about 2.3 times as wide as it is tall. 900 x 600 px.
```

## 2. `souvenirs.png`: what he brings back (5 in a row)

Attach `room-day.png`.

```text
Watercolor illustration in exactly the style of the attached room: soft paper
texture, thin brown outlines, muted warm colors. Draw 5 small objects side by
side in ONE row, evenly spaced, each inside its own fifth of the canvas with
generous transparent space around it, all at a similar size:
1. a smooth grey pebble shaped a bit like a bread roll,
2. a sparkly old bottle cap,
3. a single red autumn leaf, slightly curled,
4. a bookmark with a pressed flower,
5. a small soft feather.
Each object on its own: no shadow, no surface, no text. Transparent background.
1800 x 400 px.
```

## 3. `postcards.png`: postcards he sends mid-trip (5 in a row)

These land in your keepsakes while he's out ("Rusty sent you a postcard from the creek"). Attach `room-day.png` and
`art/puppet/neutral.webp`.

```text
Five small watercolor postcard illustrations side by side in one row, each a
landscape 3:2 rectangle with a thin cream border, in exactly the style of the
attached room (soft paper texture, thin brown outlines, muted warm colors).
In every postcard the same small orange tabby cat from the second image (same
face, stripes and colors) is a SMALL figure in the scene, seen from a little
distance, enjoying the place:
1. a shallow creek with smooth pebbles, the cat crouched looking at the water,
2. a cozy café window with a second cat (grey) inside, the cat outside on the sill,
3. a park path covered in red and orange leaves, the cat mid-pounce on a leaf,
4. a library reading corner with a radiator, the cat curled on a cushion,
5. a farmers market stall with vegetables, the cat sniffing a basket.
No text, no stamps, no handwriting. Even spacing, no overlap. 2400 x 420 px.
```

## 4. `rusty-backpack.webp`: Rusty heading out / coming back

This one is a puppet pose, so it must line up exactly with his other poses. Make it like the puppet frames: an edit
of his sitting drawing. Attach `art/puppet/neutral.webp`.

```text
Edit this image. Keep the cat EXACTLY the same: same size, position, pose,
face, eyes, stripes, colors, outline and watercolor texture, same cream
background, same canvas. Add only a tiny sage-green canvas backpack on his
back with one strap visible over his chest, and a small brown leaf tucked in
the top pocket. Nothing else changes.
```

## 5. `souvenir-shelf.png` (optional, P2): a shelf that fills up over months

Attach `room-day.png`.

```text
A small wooden wall shelf in exactly the watercolor style of the attached room
(honey wood, thin brown outlines), two tiers, empty, seen straight on, about
as wide as the desk in the room. Transparent background, no wall, no shadow.
1200 x 700 px.
```
